## House definitions

The canonical list is `dbt_repo/models/HOUSE_DEFINITIONS.md` (D1–D10). The
decisions below were confirmed in the `/truequery init` interview on
2026-10-25 and take precedence where the code disagrees.

- **Revenue (D1 + D5)**: sum of `net_usd` over orders that are canonical
  `completed`, have ≥1 succeeded payment, are **not test** (see below), and
  belong to a **non-deleted** customer. Net = gross − `processed` refunds,
  in USD. Soft-deleted customers' orders appear only in labeled audit columns
  (`deleted_gross_usd`).
- **Test / internal accounts**: a row is test if **any** signal says so —
  `customers.is_test`, `orders.is_test`, or email domain `@example.com` /
  `@internal.corp` (`stg_customers.is_test_domain`). As of 2026-10-25 the three
  agree (25 customers, 108 orders), but models must not rely on that.
- **Money (D7)**: `*_cents` are integer minor units; divide by 100 for
  USD/EUR/GBP and by **1 for JPY**, then × `fx_rates.rate_to_usd` on the
  business date. The `cents_to_dollars` / `usd_from_cents` macros always divide
  by 100 and are JPY-unsafe — don't use them.
- **Business date (D4)**: order day = `ordered_at` truncated to the **UTC**
  day. Anything named `*_utc` must be a UTC day. FX joins use the same day.

## Known deviations (confirmed against warehouse data, 2026-10-25)

These are data-level findings (evidence: `data`, from my queries, not the
checker). The marts are out of spec until they're fixed.

1. **Timezone**: `build_marts.py` sets `TimeZone='America/Los_Angeles'`, and
   `date_trunc('day', ordered_at)` on a `TIMESTAMPTZ` follows the session zone,
   so `order_day_utc` (and `paid_day_utc`, `refund_day_utc`, FX join days) are
   LA days. 872 of 2,571 orders (34%) land on the wrong day. Violates D4.
2. **Deleted customers in revenue**: `fct_orders.is_revenue_eligible` doesn't
   check `customer_is_deleted`, so $53,158.22 of $205,235.54 revenue (112
   orders) comes from soft-deleted customers. Violates D1/D5 as decided above.
3. **Test signal inconsistency**: `fct_orders` uses only `customers.is_test`;
   `dim_customers` uses only `orders.is_test`. Harmless today, wrong under the
   any-flag rule if the signals drift apart.
